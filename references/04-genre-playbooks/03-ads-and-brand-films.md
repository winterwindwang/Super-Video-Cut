# Advertising and Brand Film Editing Playbook

> Category: Genre playbooks | Topic: Feed advertising / brand TVC / corporate films / multi-version cutdowns | Compiled: 2026-11-10 | Applies to: Brands, advertising post-production, media buyers, e-commerce creative teams

> **Numeric disclaimer**: Every placement aspect ratio, resolution, duration, file size, copy length and cover dimension is "subject to the platform's latest official documentation" and must be re-verified in the ad console before launch. Conflicting figures or rules of thumb are marked TO VERIFY.
>
> **Compliance disclaimer**: This document offers compliance **guidance** only. For the full list of prohibited terms and the complete regulatory wording, "see the 05-copyright category." Every live campaign must pass legal review.

---

## 1. Genre goals and evaluation metrics

**Goal**: In an environment that is extremely short and often skipped or muted, move the viewer through "attention → comprehension → preference → action." Brand films lean toward image and recall; performance ads lean toward clicks and conversion. The two make opposite trade-offs on information density.

| Metric | Meaning | Related editing decision |
|---|---|---|
| First 5-second retention / 2-second attention | Whether the ad is seen before the skip button appears | Brand and hook must be up front |
| Brand recall | Can viewers name the brand afterwards | Persistent corner logo + opening and closing end card |
| Message hierarchy achieved | Is the single core proposition remembered | 1 film, 1 proposition |
| Completion rate / watch time | Share of full views | Pacing and music |
| Click-through rate (CTR) | Clicks / impressions | CTA, first frame, subtitles |
| CVR / CPA / ROAS | Cost per conversion and return | Landing-page consistency, CTA strength |
| A/B win rate | Outcome of the controlled test | Single-variable version matrix |

> Core constraint: **the shorter the runtime, the harder the message hierarchy must be cut back to a single point.** A 6-second bumper has room for "one proposition + one brand impression" and nothing else; cramming in the entire content of a 30-second TVC is the most common failure.

## 3. Structure template (30s / 15s / 6s cutdown timelines)

**Shoot and cut one master, then derive three versions**, stripping back layer by layer along "proposition → evidence → brand → action":

| Timeline | 30-second master | 15-second cutdown | 6-second bumper |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | Strong hook + brand up front | Hook and brand in the same frame | Single product shot + brand name |
| 2–5s | Pain point / benefit stated directly | Single selling point | One tagline |
| 5–12s | Demo point 1 + evidence | Demo and evidence compressed into 1 block | Logo + one action line / URL |
| 12–20s | Demo points 2–3 | CTA moved forward | — |
| 20–26s | Trust signals / promotion / data citation | Logo + end card | — |
| 26–30s | CTA + logo end card ≥2–3 s | — | — |

**Message hierarchy and brand exposure duration** (suggested values, TO VERIFY):

| Layer | 30s | 15s | 6s |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single core proposition | 1, repeated ≤3 times | 1 | 1 |
| Supporting evidence / demo | 2–3 | 1 | 0 |
| CTA | Final 3–4 s + voice-over | Final 2–3 s | Final 1–2 s |
| Brand exposure | Corner logo throughout + ≥3 s at head and tail | Corner logo + end card ≥2 s | Throughout or ≥2 s at the end |

> CTA triad: a spoken CTA (once within the first 10 seconds is recommended) + an on-screen button or text + a matching landing page. In vertical, keep key visuals inside the central clean zone, place the logo in the top or left safe area, and avoid the bottom description and buy button (see category 01).

**A/B version matrix** (change exactly 1 variable per version):

```
Variable 1 Hook: "suspense" vs "benefit" vs "conflict"
Variable 2 First frame: "product close-up" vs "facial expression" vs "before/after comparison"
Variable 3 CTA: "Buy now" vs "Learn more" vs "Claim for a limited time"
Variable 4 Music: "hard beat-synced" vs "emotional bed"
Variable 5 Duration: 30s vs 15s vs 6s
```

## 4. Key techniques and specific parameters

| Item | 30-second master | 15-second | 6-second bumper |
|---|---|---|---|
| Average shot length | 1.5–3 s | 1–2 s | 1–2 shots for the whole film |
| Cut density in the first 5 seconds | 1 cut every 1–2 s | 1 cut every 1 second | A single or double cut |
| Total shot count (reference) | 10–18 | 6–10 | 1–3 |
| Subtitles | ≤12 characters per screen; enlarge selling points, price and CTA with kinetic text | ≤10 characters | ≤8 characters |
| Music | Lock the final mix before cutting; snap cuts to strong beats | Chorus up front | One heavy hit / sound effect |
| Loudness | About −14 LUFS, −1 dBTP (TO VERIFY), ducking under the voice | Same as left | Same as left |

**Cutdown workflow**: Finish the 30-second horizontal master first (keep a ProRes/H.264 master) → derive the 15-second (drop the evidence layer, move the CTA forward) → derive the 6-second (keep only the strongest visual + one line of copy + logo) → finally build the 9:16 vertical and Shorts versions (reframe, subject centered). **Don't re-edit each version from scratch, and don't shove the horizontal cut into a vertical frame** (cropping 16:9 to 9:16 loses about 68% of the width).

**Brand exposure**: Corner logo throughout + end cards at head and tail (≥2–3 s, carrying logo, slogan, URL/QR code). A skippable ad must complete at least one clear brand impression before the skip button appears at second 5.

## 5. Audio and subtitle specifics

1. **Satisfy both "audio required" and "readable when muted"**: WeChat Channels ad policy explicitly requires that the video "must have audio," yet feed placements often autoplay muted. Price, selling points and CTA must stand on their own through subtitles and picture. The test: watch it muted and see whether the proposition and the action still land.
2. **Disclaimers / legal text** (a compliance priority): Never evade visibility with tiny type, pale color, blurred watermarks, collapsed sections or a flash frame. Type size must be in reasonable proportion to the main copy, color must contrast clearly with the background, and dwell time must be adequate. Never "big type for the hook, tiny type for the disclaimer."
3. **Ad labeling**: Internet promotional content — short video, image-text, livestream — must be clearly labeled "advertisement" in accordance with the law (guidance only; see category 05).
4. **Cited content**: Data, statistics, survey results and quotations must be true and accurate, with sources cited; any scope of application and validity period must be stated explicitly; fabricated or unverifiable statistics are prohibited.
5. **Music and fonts**: Commercial campaigns must use music, fonts, images and likenesses with explicit commercial licensing, and keep the paperwork. Music built into a platform is usually licensed for use on that platform only and does not automatically cover paid media, a corporate website or cross-platform use (see category 05).

## 6. Common pitfalls and anti-patterns (compliance red lines flagged as guidance only)

| Pitfall | Anti-pattern | Consequence | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Copying a long cut into a short one | Squeezing a 30-second TVC into 6 seconds | Information overload; nothing is remembered | Keep 1 proposition + 1 brand + 1 action |
| Brand shown too late | Logo only at the end | Not seen before the 5-second skip | Front-load in the first 2 seconds + persistent corner logo |
| Parallel propositions | One film with 3 selling points + 2 promotions | Recall drops to zero | Cut the hierarchy to three layers or fewer |
| Tiny-type disclaimers | 8px light-gray text flashing past | Misleading big-type/small-type pattern; non-compliant | Proportional type size, adequate dwell time |
| No "advertisement" label | Native-looking content that is in fact paid promotion | Non-compliant | Label it clearly |
| **Absolute claims** | "best / number one / only / national-level / top-tier / 100%" | Violates Article 9 of the Advertising Law; penalties of 200,000–1,000,000 CNY (TO VERIFY) | Switch to objective description + verifiable substantiation |
| Open-ended urgency / inducement | "Ends anytime," "last batch," "click for a surprise," "flash sale," "selling out" | Non-compliant | State specific campaign dates / real terms |
| Authority endorsement | "Recommended by a government body," "exclusive supply," "quality inspection exempt" | Non-compliant | Remove; never borrow official authority |
| Landing-page mismatch | A vertical clip linking to a different product | False advertising / unfair competition | Product and voice-over must match exactly |
| A/B with several variables at once | Changing hook, music and CTA together | No attribution possible | One variable per version |
| Hard-cropping horizontal to vertical | Subject cut off, text outside the frame | Footage unusable | Reframe or blur fill |

> The prohibited terms above are a **high-frequency risk reminder**, not an exhaustive list. For the complete wording, exceptions and latest amendments, "see the 05-copyright category." Every campaign must clear compliance review before launch.

## 7. Pre-delivery checklist

- [ ] The 30s / 15s / 6s versions derive from one master, with the message hierarchy stripped back at each step
- [ ] The 6-second version contains only 1 proposition + 1 brand impression + 1 action
- [ ] A skippable ad shows the brand clearly before the 5-second skip; corner logo + end card ≥2–3 s
- [ ] Aspect ratio, resolution, duration, file size and cover match the placement (re-verified against the platform's latest official documentation)
- [ ] The video has audio, and the proposition, price and CTA are still readable when muted
- [ ] All three CTA elements are present, with one spoken CTA inside the first 10 seconds
- [ ] Disclaimer type size, contrast and dwell time are compliant; no invisible or flash-frame small print
- [ ] "Advertisement" is clearly labeled; cited data states source, scope and validity period
- [ ] All copy has passed the prohibited-term check (absolute claims, urgency, inducement, authority endorsement, false exaggeration) — full list in category 05
- [ ] Music, fonts, images, likenesses and archive material have commercial clearance on file
- [ ] Overall about −14 LUFS, −1 dBTP (re-verified against the platform's latest official documentation)
- [ ] The vertical version keeps the subject centered and clears the bottom description and right-side button safe areas
- [ ] Each A/B version changes exactly 1 variable, with naming and records traceable
- [ ] Legal / compliance review passed and archived

# Stock Assets and Learning Resources

> Category: Tools and resources | Topic: asset licensing, music and font licensing, standards documents and learning resources | Compiled: 2026-11-10 | Applies to: choosing assets for commercial projects, licence self-checks, building a team knowledge base

> **Highest-priority statement: licensing must be re-checked by you.** Before any commercial placement, open the full official licence page and confirm it clause by clause, and keep screenshots and download records; third-party summaries may be out of date, and **only the complete official licence text is legally binding**. This document does not constitute legal advice.

## 1. Core principles and concept comparison

Asset risk comes from three independent layers of rights, and **a free licence usually covers only the first**:

| Layer of rights | What it covers | Covered by a free licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Copyright | Reproduction, adaptation and distribution of the file | Usually yes (subject to the terms) |
| Likeness / privacy rights | Identifiable people in the shot | **Usually no** |
| Trademark / property rights | Logos, brands, buildings, product appearance | **Usually no** |

### Telling licence types apart

| Type | Meaning | Commercial-use notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CC0 | Public-domain dedication, the most permissive | Commercial use, modification, no attribution |
| CC-BY | Attribution only | **Attribution is mandatory** |
| CC-BY-SA | Attribution + share-alike | Derivatives must carry the same licence; viral risk |
| CC-BY-NC | Non-commercial | **Commercial use prohibited** |
| Library-specific licence | Bespoke terms from Pexels / Pixabay / Unsplash | Read the official page clause by clause |
| Royalty-free | Use within the licensed scope once authorised | **Not the same as free, and not a waiver of copyright**; check platform, territory and term |
| Sync licence | The right to synchronise music with picture | Must be obtained separately |
| Editorial use only | Editorial / news use only | **Commercial advertising prohibited** |
| Rights-managed | Priced by use, term and territory | Exceeding the scope is a breach |

**Important clarification (TO VERIFY)**: plenty of third-party articles describe Pexels / Pixabay / Unsplash as "CC0", but all three official pages use their own **Content Licence**, which differs materially from CC0 (no standalone resale, no use as a trademark). Go by the official page — **licensing must be re-checked by you**.

## 2. Actionable rules and parameter tables: verifying the official licence terms

### 2.1 Pexels License (summary of the official page)

| Permitted | Prohibited |
| --- | --- |
| Free to use, commercial use allowed, **no attribution required**, modification allowed | Identifiable people must not be placed in a negative or offensive context |
| — | Unmodified copies may not be sold as they are (posters, prints, physical goods) |
| — | Must not imply that a person or brand endorses a product |
| — | Must not be redistributed or sold on other stock or wallpaper platforms |
| — | Must not be used as a trademark, trade name or service mark |

### 2.2 Pixabay Content License (summary of the official page)

| Permitted | Prohibited (Prohibited Uses) |
| --- | --- |
| Free to use, **no attribution required**, may be adapted into new works | Standalone sale or distribution: no creative effort applied, materially the same as the original |
| — | Content containing identifiable trademarks / logos / brands must not be used commercially for goods and services, or printed on merchandise for sale |
| — | Must not be used in an unethical or unlawful way (especially where identifiable people appear), or to mislead or deceive |
| — | Must not be used as a trademark, trade name or service mark |

Two statements from the official pages are worth memorising: **only the complete version of the Content License is legally binding** (full text at pixabay.com/service/terms/); and **some content may be subject to third-party intellectual property rights (copyright, trademarks, design rights, moral rights, privacy or likeness rights and the like), so it is for the user to check whether third-party consent or a licence is required**. That second statement is the official basis for "licensing must be re-checked by you".

### 2.3 SIL Open Font License 1.1 (verified against the official full text)

| Permitted | The five conditions |
| --- | --- |
| Use, study, copy, merge, **embed**, modify, redistribute and sell copies | ① The font, or any component of it, **must not be sold on its own** |
| May be bundled with software, embedded, redistributed and sold | ② Every copy must carry the copyright notice and the full licence |
| May be used in commercial video, broadcast and printed documents | ③ Modified versions must not use the **Reserved Font Name (RFN)** without written permission |
| — | ④ The name of the copyright holder or author must not be used to endorse modified versions |
| — | ⑤ The font and its derivatives must be distributed entirely under the OFL; the licence may not be changed |

**Key exemption**: condition ⑤ explicitly does not apply to *documents created with the font*, so video, images and PDFs rendered with an OFL font can be used commercially as normal. Breaching any condition terminates the licence automatically; the font is provided AS IS, without warranty. The OFL 1.1 text is dated 2007-02-26, and the official FAQ is at version 1.1-update8 (2026-11); **version details are TO VERIFY**.

### 2.4 Licence re-check list (actionable)

- Use: commercial or non-commercial, platform, territory, term, medium, redistribution
- Content: identifiable faces, brands, trademarks, private premises, works of art
- Licence: open the **complete official** text and check it against the Prohibited Uses clause by clause
- Evidence: licence page + detail-page screenshots, download date, author, whether modified
- Music: attribution obligations, platform restrictions, sync rights, Content ID
- Fonts: licence file, the five OFL conditions, RFN, whether sold standalone
- **Conclusion: licensing must be re-checked by you**; this document does not replace the official terms or legal judgement

**Fonts are a high-risk area (TO VERIFY)**: commercial use of Microsoft YaHei (微软雅黑), the Founder (方正) range and some Source Han weights readily leads to disputes; use only fonts that are explicitly free for commercial use and for which you can obtain the licence file, and archive the licence text with the project.

**Music licensing models compared**

| Model | Meaning | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CC0 / own free library | Free, mostly no attribution | Still bound by the Prohibited Uses |
| CC-BY | Attribution only | Missing attribution, wrong format |
| Royalty-free library | Subscription or one-off licence | Out of scope, subscription expiry, cross-platform use |
| Platform library — CapCut (剪映) / YouTube | Already cleared by the platform | **Cross-platform or commercial use is often restricted** |
| Sync licence | Synchronisation rights for music with picture | Using without one is infringement |
| PRO / Content ID | Automatic detection by rights holders | Claim, muting, takedown |

## 5. Procedure and workflow (commands verified on local ffmpeg 8.1.2)

Licence self-check SOP: define the use → open the full official licence page → check each Prohibited Use → check for people and trademarks → screenshot for the record → build the ledger → review fonts and music separately → check once more before delivery.

```bash
# ① Inspect the copyright / source metadata embedded in the asset
ffprobe -v error -show_entries format_tags -of json asset.mp4

# ② Batch-generate the asset list (resolution, frame rate, duration, size)
for f in assets/*.mp4; do
  echo "== $f =="
  ffprobe -v error -show_entries format=duration,size:stream=codec_name,width,height,r_frame_rate -of csv "$f"
done

# ③ Extract the first frame as a ledger thumbnail, for evidential use
ffmpeg -y -ss 0 -i asset.mp4 -frames:v 1 -q:v 2 thumbs/asset.jpg

# ④ The OFL requires the full licence text to be distributed with copies of the font
mkdir -p deliver/fonts && cp fonts/OFL.txt deliver/fonts/
```

Ledger fields: `source URL | author | site | licence type | licence page URL | download date | modified? | credit text | placement platform | screenshot path`.

## 6. Common errors and troubleshooting

| Symptom | Root cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assuming "free for commercial use means no liability" | Likeness and trademark rights ignored | Check each Prohibited Use; obtain separate consent for people and brands |
| Treating Pexels/Pixabay as CC0 | Out-of-date second-hand articles (TO VERIFY) | Go by the official licence page |
| Printing the asset as it is on merchandise and selling it | Breaches the Standalone prohibition | Make a substantive modification first, or change licence |
| Using footage containing a logo in a product ad | Trademark rights are outside the licence | Change the asset or obtain brand authorisation |
| An identifiable person appears in a negative context | Explicitly prohibited by Pexels | Change the asset |
| Used a CC-BY-NC track from Freesound commercially | Individual licences not checked | Keep only CC0 / CC-BY |
| Forgot attribution for Uppbeat / Freepik | The free tier carries an attribution obligation | Credit it in the description or end credits as the licence requires |
| Sold an OFL font on its own | Breaches condition ① | Bundle it only with software or a work |
| Modified an OFL font and kept the original name | Breaches the RFN condition ③ | Rename the font |
| Assumed the OFL is viral on the finished film | Misread condition ⑤ | The official text explicitly excludes "documents created with the font" |
| Received a claim over Microsoft YaHei / Founder fonts | Unclear licensing (TO VERIFY) | Switch to a font that is clearly free for commercial use and archive the licence |
| Platform-library music posted to another platform | Licence limited to one platform | Re-read the terms or change the track |
| Downloaded a Sponsored paid image by mistake | Aggregator pages mix in advertising | Open the detail page and confirm the free marker |

## 7. Quick checklist

- [ ] **Licensing re-checked by you**: the complete official licence page has been opened; nothing is taken from a third-party summary
- [ ] Use, platform, territory, term and redistribution all written down
- [ ] Identifiable people, brands, trademarks and property rights checked separately
- [ ] Licence type determined (CC0 / CC-BY / library-specific / editorial-only / subscription)
- [ ] Prohibited Uses checked one by one, especially standalone resale and the trademark prohibition
- [ ] Music: attribution, sync rights, platform restrictions and Content ID confirmed
- [ ] Fonts: licence file, the five OFL conditions, RFN and "must not be sold on its own" confirmed
- [ ] Ledger, licence-page and detail-page screenshots, and download dates archived
- [ ] Aggregator listings opened down to the individual licence page
- [ ] Every clause marked TO VERIFY re-checked with the official source; high-risk placements cleared by legal
